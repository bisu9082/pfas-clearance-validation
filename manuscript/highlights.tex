\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item The most-sensitive aquatic endpoint correlates with testing effort at $\rho=-0.73$
\item Half of that association is a sampling-depth artefact, not chemistry
\item A fixed model gives $R^2=-0.75$ on the minimum and $+0.22$ on an acute median
\item Rebuilding on an effort-stable endpoint halves the 90\% prediction interval
\item No untested PFAS clears at a stringency-matched hazard threshold even so
\end{itemize}
