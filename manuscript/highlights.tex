\noindent\textbf{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item The most-sensitive aquatic endpoint correlates with testing effort at $\rho=-0.75$
\item Depth equalisation removes 40--48\% of it: an extreme-order-statistic artefact
\item Swapping only the endpoint raises rank transfer by $\Delta\rho=+0.32$ on 58 PFAS
\item An effort-stable endpoint narrows the 90\% prediction interval from 10.5 to 4.5
\item No untested PFAS clears at a stringency-matched hazard threshold even so
\end{itemize}
